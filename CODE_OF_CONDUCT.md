# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
viewpoints, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `leonidtimo17@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and fairly. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code.
